    Use your phone in Google Meet

    One-time setup of the PhoneCam Chrome extension:

    1. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
    2. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder phonecam\extension\dist.
    3. Pin PhoneCam from the puzzle-piece menu so its status is always visible.

    Then in every class:

    1. Start PhoneCam on this PC (start.bat) and on your phone.
    2. In Meet: ⋮ → Settings → Video → Camera → PhoneCam.
    3. Optional: Audio → Microphone → PhoneCam Microphone.

    Want to check it first? Open the meeting test page.
